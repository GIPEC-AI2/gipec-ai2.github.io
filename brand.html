How Brand Safety works

1
Add the client

Click + New client. Give it a codename (for example “Client CPG-1”), the company name, and every product brand to watch. Only the codename shows on screens and reports.

2
SPEAR scans

Click Scan now, or turn monitoring on to scan on a schedule. SPEAR searches public Bluesky posts, the public Telegram channels on your watchlist and the web for the brand names. Its AI checks each post using the codebook and team memory. News, research and counter-speech are set aside.

3
You decide

Flagged posts land in To review. Open the post, add a note, then Confirm or Reject. Nothing reaches the client unless you confirm it. For things you capture yourself, like the brand's ad next to extremist content on Meta, use Add an incident you found on the Confirmed tab.

4
Send the report

Open Client report, pick the period and click Print / save as PDF. Download the exclusion list: the accounts, hashtags and coded terms the brand should block in its ad settings.

Two kinds of findings: Names the brand (extremist or hateful posts that use or attack the brand) and In brand ad context (harmful posts inside the brand's campaign hashtags or accounts). Public sources only: no logins, no fake accounts, no private content.
